Designed by renowned architect Koji Tanaka, this estate represents a masterclass in Brutalist minimalism. Every surface is an intentional dialogue between raw cast concrete and the expansive horizon of the Pacific Ocean. The structure is carved into the hillside, ensuring total privacy while maintaining a seamless connection with the natural landscape.

The interior is defined by twenty-foot ceilings and floor-to-ceiling glass panels that disappear into the walls, transforming the living spaces into open-air pavilions. Hand-selected travertine floors and blackened steel accents provide a subtle tonal contrast to the warm, morning light that floods the gallery-inspired hallways.
